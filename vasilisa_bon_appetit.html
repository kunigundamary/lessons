Act 4

🧊 some & any

✅ some
в утверждении
There is some milk.
There are some eggs.
❓🚫 any
в вопросе и отрицании
Is there any milk?
There aren't any apples.
Вежливое исключение. Когда предлагаем или просим, говорим some даже в вопросе:
Would you like some tea? Can I have some water, please?

Act 5

⚖️ much, many, a lot of

manyто, что считаем
How many apples?
I don't have many biscuits.
muchто, что не считаем
How much water?
There isn't much milk.
a lot ofи то, и другое
I eat a lot of apples.
I drink a lot of water.
Подсказка. Much и many чаще живут в вопросах и отрицаниях. В обычном утверждении удобнее a lot of. А про деньги: How much is it? Сколько стоит? It's three pounds.

Choose much, many or a lot of

Act 6

📬 Emily's lunch

Эмили рассказывает, что едят в ее балетной школе. Прочитай. Потом нажми «Покажи цвета».

Hi Vasilisa!

You asked about food at my school. Dancers need a lot of energy, so we eat well!

For breakfast I usually have a bowl of porridge with some honey and a banana. I don't drink any coffee, but I love a cup of tea.

At lunch we go to the canteen. There are a lot of vegetables, and there is always some soup, rice or pasta. I don't eat much meat, but I like fish.

Our teacher says we mustn't eat a lot of sweets. Is there any chocolate in my bag right now? Yes, there is: a small bar of chocolate for after class! Shh!

In my snack box there is an apple, some nuts and a bottle of water. What's in your snack box? How many lessons do you have before lunch?

Love,
Emily
