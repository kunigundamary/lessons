Act 2

✏️ How to make -ing

Present Continuous: am / is / are + глагол с окончанием -ing. Но у -ing есть свои хитрости.

+ ingstretch → stretching
e исчезаетdance → dancing
двойная букваsit → sitting

Write the -ing form

I am, she is, they are

I am (I'm) dancing.
She is (she's) dancing.
They are (they're) dancing.
I'm not dancing.
She isn't dancing.
Is she dancing? Yes, she is.

Hi Vasilisa!

I'm writing to you from backstage! Usually on Saturdays I have ballet class in the morning, and in the afternoon I go home. But today is different: we are performing The Nutcracker!

Right now the older students are dancing on stage. I'm sitting in the dressing room and I'm waiting for my turn. My friend Lily is doing her make-up, and our teacher is checking our costumes. I'm very nervous!

Backstage there are a lot of rules. We must be very quiet. We mustn't touch the props, and we mustn't eat in our costumes. We have to be ready fifteen minutes before our dance. We don't have to stay after the show, so I can go home with my mum.

Oh! The music is starting. I have to go!

Write back soon and tell me: what are you doing now? What rules do you have at your Academy?

Love,

Act 5

📜 Rules: must, mustn't, have to

✅ must / have to
надо, обязательно
You must be on time.
We have to wear a bun.
🚫 mustn't
нельзя!
You mustn't run in the corridor.
😌 don't have to = не обязательно, можно не делать. Это совсем не то же самое, что mustn't!
It's Sunday. I don't have to get up early. (можно поспать)
I mustn't be late for class. (нельзя опаздывать)
А про нее и про него: she has to, she doesn't have to. У must окончания нет: she must.
